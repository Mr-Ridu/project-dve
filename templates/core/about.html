About {{ company.name }}

Equipment and systems for five industrial sectors, backed by service and technical expertise.

We help industry specify, install and run the equipment its processes depend on.

{{ company.name }} supplies process equipment, plant machinery, power systems, metal processing equipment and automation for manufacturers across Bangladesh. Every system is specified against the process it has to serve, not simply sold from a catalogue.

Our portfolio covers five sectors: Industrial Plant & Machinery, Energy & Power, Robotic & Automation, Steel & Metal, and Pharma & Biotech. Within each, we work with the process owner and the engineering team to agree capacity, utilities, layout and the support package before anything is ordered.

What we supply

  • Industrial Plant & Machinery
    Processing, handling and line equipment built around your throughput.
  • Energy & Power
    Generation, distribution and balance-of-plant equipment.
  • Robotic & Automation
    Robotic cells and controls integrated with the existing plant.
  • Steel & Metal
    Heavy-duty equipment for melting, rolling and finishing lines.
  • Pharma & Biotech
    Process equipment and cleanroom systems for regulated production.

How we work

We start with the process, the site and the uptime you need. From there we specify the equipment, plan the installation around your schedule, commission it with your team and stay available for maintenance, spares and technical advice.

A single point of contact follows the project from first enquiry through to after-sales support, so the people who specified the system are the people you call when you need help.
